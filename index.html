About

I work on superconducting quantum technologies in Milan. My research looks at qudits as a natural extension of qubits, with applications to fermionic simulation and to searches for dark-matter candidates such as axions and dark photons.

On the experimental side I design control and readout experiments for superconducting devices, and I build the RFSoC-based electronics and FPGA firmware that run them. I contribute to the open-source quantum-control projects Qibolab and Qibosoq.
