Subject

Superconducting qubits & qudits, and the FPGA electronics that drive them

About

I’m a PhD candidate in Physics at the University of Milano-Bicocca, working on superconducting quantum devices.

My research looks at qudits as a natural extension of qubits, for fermionic simulation and for searches of dark-matter candidates such as axions and dark photons. In the lab I run control and readout experiments, and I build what runs them: RFSoC firmware, FPGA lock-in amplifiers and open-source control software like Qibosoq and Qibolab.

Before and during the PhD I worked at the Technology Innovation Institute in Abu Dhabi and at Intermodulation Products in Stockholm. If you are hiring, or you are a student looking for a thesis on qubits, qudits or FPGAs, write to me.
